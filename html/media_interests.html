Media I'm interested in

I have a tendency to become obsessed with pieces of media and dive way too deep into them so heres a few of my absolute favourites. These pages are also somewhat shrines to these media.

Whitepine is a period drama set in minecraft made by IvoryTV on youtube and oh my god it is the most beautiful, most gut wrenching, most heartfelt Piece of media I've ever had the pleasure of consuming. If you ever have the time you need to watch this show the story is interesting the visuals are on point (Its in minecraft and looks better than some live action movies I've seen and definitely plenty of animated ones) and the music, oh the music.

Murder drones is a sci-fi horror-comedy series made by Liam Vickers and produced by Glitch production (The guys who produced The Amazing Digital Circus) Murder Drones as a show is just so much fun while also managing to be cute, terrifying and gripping at the same time. The voice acting in is incredible and while the animation isn't the most incredible in the beginning by the last episode it is truly something to behold.

Lackadaisy is an animated series set in a feline populated St. Louis in 1927 during the prohibition and follows the Lackadaisy speakeasy which was once a prestigious establishment but since the death of its owner Atlas, its leadership has fallen to to Mitzi May his wife who is struggling to keep the business afloat in the first and currently only episode we follow Rocky, Freckle and Ivy as they attempt to smuggle illegal alcohol for Lackadaisy. Fun show, Cool action and stunning visuals.

Port by the sea is an animated series that follows Port and Umi as they sail the seas to rebuild the moon which has been shattered into many pieces. Only the pilot for this series is out at the time of writing but I'm already in love with the series.. And they use Miracle Musicals Introduction to the snow in the end scene so it instantly gets my vote.
